Atelier · Zürich Old Town

A small atelier
by choice.

One room, one bench, tall windows and the Old Town beyond them.

Atelier Benvenisti makes a limited number of handbags in Zürich. Everything we sell is made in this building, by the person who signs it off.

There is no second site, no subcontractor and no finishing partner. What arrives here as a hide leaves as a finished bag.

What we hold to

01 · The room

A single workshop in the Old Town: one long bench under the windows, hides on the rack, tools that have not changed in a century and do not need to.

02 · Zürich

The city is not decoration on a label. It is why the work looks the way it does: exact, unhurried, unshowy, and expected to hold up. Hides arrive here from Tuscany, patterns are drawn here, and every finished piece is packed and posted from this address.

03 · The standard

Full-grain vegetable-tanned leather, hand-cut components, hand-set gold-plated hardware. If a piece does not pass the final check it does not ship, and the making time starts again.

The maker

Every bag leaves here signed.

Atelier Benvenisti is one person at one bench. I select the hides, draw the patterns, cut, stitch and finish each piece myself, and I am the last person to check it before it is packed.

Nothing is made in advance. Work on your bag begins the day your payment clears — from that moment it is yours, and no one else's hands touch it.

Entirely by hand

No machine seam, no production line.

Begun on payment

Cutting starts once your order is settled.

Signed and numbered

Marked by the maker, not a factory.

On staying small

We could make more. We would have to stop making them this way.

The number of bags that leave here in a year is set by the hours in a week, not by demand. We would rather turn down an order than hand one to a second pair of hands.

Leather that changes with you.

Full-grain, vegetable-tanned, from Tuscany. Tanned by methods refined over centuries, and free of synthetic surface coatings.

The natural grain, texture and character stay visible. Variations in grain and colour belong to the material itself, which makes each bag slightly different from the last.

With use, the leather becomes softer, deeper in colour and develops a rich patina while keeping its strength and structure. Rather than simply showing its age, it becomes more beautiful through it.
